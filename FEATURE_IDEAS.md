# TinyWins Feature Ideas

## High Value

- Edit existing habits.
- Current streak and best streak tracking.
- Weekly and monthly completion analytics.
- Flexible schedules, including weekdays-only habits.
- Reminders and scheduled notifications.
- Archive habits without deleting their history.

## Logging Improvements

- Edit or undo individual logs.
- Add notes or mood to each entry.
- Keyboard shortcuts for quickly recording habits.
- Better daily history and calendar views.

## Organization

- Habit categories and tags.
- Search and filtering.
- Drag-and-drop habit ordering.
- Custom dashboard sections.

## Insights and Motivation

- Weekly review summaries.
- Personal milestones and celebrations.
- Consistency trends and missed-day recovery guidance.
- Goal recommendations based on past activity.

## Data and Integrations

- Export history as CSV or JSON.
- Import existing habit data.
- Calendar integration.
- Home-screen or desktop widgets.
